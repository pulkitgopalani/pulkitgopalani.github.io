I am a third year PhD candidate in [Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/) at University of Michigan (Ann Arbor) advised by [Prof. Wei Hu](https://weihu.me) and [Prof. Gabriel Poesia](https://gpoesia.com/). My research focuses on the science of deep learning, specifically training dynamics and reasoning capabilities of Transformers.

I completed my undergraduate studies (BTech) at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur, majoring in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science & Engineering.
